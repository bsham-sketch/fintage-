Fintage Legacy

Your wealth. Your wishes. Your legacy.

You may know what you have built. The more difficult question is what happens to it when you are no longer able to manage it yourself. Fintage Legacy helps individuals organise their affairs, articulate their wishes and create a framework designed to ensure those wishes are carried forward.

The Legacy journey

1. UNDERSTAND

Create a clear inventory of assets, obligations, relationships and existing documents.

2. ARTICULATE

Document beneficiaries, personal wishes, charitable intentions and priorities.

3. COORDINATE

Work with independent lawyers, accountants, trustees, investment and property professionals as required.

4. PREPARE

Put appropriate lifetime and later-life arrangements in place.

5. EXECUTE

Coordinate the transition to executors / trustees / administrators when required.

6. CONTINUE

Carry forward the intended legacy, including philanthropic structures where chosen.

You decide what happens to your wealth. Fintage’s role is to help ensure that decision is clearly planned and professionally carried forward.

For individuals without a natural successor; families whose next generation lives abroad; business owners with complex succession needs; and anyone who wants greater certainty about how their affairs will ultimately be handled.
