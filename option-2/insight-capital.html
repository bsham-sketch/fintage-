A longer view of capital

Decisions rarely sit in isolation. A capital choice can shape a business, a wealth decision can affect a family, and succession planning can carry intentions into a future that is still taking shape.

A useful starting point is to name the decision, understand the people and interests around it, and make space to consider more than one horizon. That context can help clarify what matters and which questions deserve attention.

The right approach will depend on individual circumstances. Independent professional advice may be appropriate before acting.
